It doesn't touch the score. `icp_target` says who you sell to, and that doesn't change because a campaign is running. What it adds is the **second question** — how much of this creator's audience is where the campaign is actually running — as its own sentence on every card.

          None of this audience is in the campaign's regions.

Two different reasons to say no. A brand selling across the EU can run a campaign in Germany alone, so a strong ICP match can reach almost nobody there — one merged number would have hidden that they disagree.
