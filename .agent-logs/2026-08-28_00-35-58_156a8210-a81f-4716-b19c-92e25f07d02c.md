That's the number arriving at the same verdict the post page reaches from the other end.

Four decisions worth your eye:

**`largestDetractor` picks the dimension costing the most points, not the worst overlap** — a total miss on a light dimension matters less than a partial miss on a heavy one. That run exposed a real tie: industry and geo both lost exactly 25.0. Either answer is true, so I documented that the comparison keeps `SCORE_DIMENSIONS` order, making it deterministic rather than dependent on facet load order.

**A share outside 0..1 throws** rather than being treated as zero. Scoring around broken input yields a confident number off bad data. Summed overlap is clamped to 1, since four-decimal shares can total a hair over as floats.

**The breakdown carries an `observed` flag.** A snapshot with no facets for a dimension gives overlap 0 — arithmetically identical to a total miss, but it means *no data*, not bad data.

**Two refactors I made without being asked:** `scorePerson` moved to `src/lib/score/person.ts` so `weights.ts` is constants only, and the score module now uses explicit `.ts` import specifiers. The second one wasn't cosmetic — the engine wouldn't load under plain Node, and `seed-demo` already imports from this module. I re-ran the demo seed afterwards to confirm it still produces identical output.
